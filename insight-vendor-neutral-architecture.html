Sourcing strategy

Why vendor-neutral architecture wins

When the architect earns reseller margin, the architecture serves the margin.

Most backup and DR proposals come from someone who also sells the software or the hardware. That's not automatically a conflict — but it means every recommendation carries a second incentive alongside "what's right for this environment."

A vendor-neutral specialist matches the tool to the workload: requirements, security, scalability and best practice decide, not quotas or reseller margin. That's why DeeAROps carries no allegiance to a single software vendor, platform or cloud provider — the right backup platform for a Veeam-friendly VMware estate is not the right one for an Oracle-heavy, Data Guard-dependent one, and a vendor-neutral architect is free to say so.

It also means the relationship doesn't end at the sale. Being vendor-neutral is what lets DeeAROps stay accountable to the recovery outcome — tested, documented, monitored — rather than to a single vendor's roadmap.
